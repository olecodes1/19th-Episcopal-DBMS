# AME Church Organizational Structure

## Overview
The African Methodist Episcopal (AME) Church is a hierarchical connectional church with governance structures from local to international levels.

## Hierarchical Structure

### 1. General Conference (Connectional Level)
- Highest governing body
- Meets quadrennially (every 4 years)
- Composed of Bishops (ex-officio presidents) and equal ministerial and lay delegates
- Next Quadrennial: 2027

### 2. Episcopal District Level
- **19th Episcopal District** (South Africa)
- Led by Presiding Bishop
- Oversees multiple Annual Conferences
- District-level officers and departments

### 3. Annual Conference Level
Five Conferences in 19th Episcopal District:
1. **Mokone Conference** (MM - Mangena Maake)
2. **Orangia Conference**
3. **Natal Conference**
4. **West Conference**
5. **East Conference**

### 4. Area Level
- Each Conference divided into Areas (e.g., Mokone has 13 Areas)
- Area leadership: President and Director
- Coordinates multiple local churches/circuits

### 5. Local Church/Circuit Level
- Individual congregations
- Local church officers and departments
- Base level of church organization

## Key Departments & Organizations

### Young People's Department (YPD)
**Structure:** Operates at all levels (Connectional → District → Conference → Area → Local)

**Age Divisions:**
- **Mother Sunbeams**: Ages 2-6 years
- **Allen Stars**: Ages 7-12 years
- **Youth Auxiliary**: Ages 13-17 years
- **Young Adults**: Ages 18-26 years



## Governance & Representation

### Electoral Structure
- **Lay Electoral Colleges**: Elect lay delegates to Annual and General Conferences
- **Voting Eligibility**: 
  - Conference level voting
  - Episcopal level voting
  - Based on membership status and participation

### Officers & Committees
Each level has standardized officer positions including:
- President/Presiding Elder
- Secretary
- Treasurer
- Historiographer & Statistician
- Various department directors

## Data & Reporting Flow

```
Local Church → Area → Conference → Episcopal District → Connectional
```

**Reporting Frequency:** Quarterly submissions required
**Key Data:**
- Membership enrollment
- Attendance statistics
- Financial reports
- Historical documentation
- Event summaries

## AME Church Mission
- Christian evangelism
- Social justice and community service
- Education and leadership development
- Historical preservation
- Global connectional unity
